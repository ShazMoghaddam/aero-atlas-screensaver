═══════════════════════════════════════════════
 macOS INSTALLATION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Double-click "Aero Atlas — Terminal Time-1.0.0.dmg"

2. Drag the "Aero Atlas — Terminal Time" app into your
   Applications folder when the installer window appears.

3. IMPORTANT — First Launch (Gatekeeper):
   macOS may say the app "cannot be opened because it is from
   an unidentified developer." This is normal for indie apps.

   To open it anyway:
   a) Right-click (or Control-click) the app icon
   b) Choose "Open" from the menu
   c) Click "Open" in the dialog that appears
   You only need to do this once.

4. The screensaver launches fullscreen immediately.
   Press Esc or move your mouse to close it.

5. To set it as your system screensaver:
   • This app runs as a standalone fullscreen screensaver app.
   • To auto-launch it after idle time, use a free tool like:
       – Lungo (Mac App Store, free)
       – Alfred workflow
       – Or simply add it to your Login Items and use
         System Settings → Lock Screen → "Start Screen Saver after"
         (set to your preferred idle time, then set the screensaver
          to a system one — Terminal Time will overlay on demand)
   • The simplest approach: press the app's icon in your Dock
     whenever you want the clock display.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════
 WINDOWS INSTALLATION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Double-click "Aero Atlas — Terminal Time Setup.exe"

2. Follow the installer steps. You can choose your install folder.

3. Once installed, find "Aero Atlas — Terminal Time" in your
   Start Menu or Desktop shortcut.

4. The screensaver launches fullscreen. Press Esc or move your
   mouse to close it.

5. To set as Windows screensaver:
   a) Right-click the Desktop → Personalize
   b) Click "Lock screen" → "Screen saver settings"
   c) Click Browse and navigate to:
        C:\Program Files\Aero Atlas — Terminal Time\
      Select "Aero Atlas - Terminal Time.exe"
   d) Set your idle timeout and click OK.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════
 USING THE SCREENSAVER
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

CONTROLS (move mouse first to show the toolbar):

  • Click the settings icon to:
      – Add or remove cities (up to 10)
      – Change the accent colour theme
      – Toggle click sounds on/off

  • Drag city rows to reorder them

  • Your city (detected automatically) always appears first

  • Press Esc or move mouse to exit screensaver mode


KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS:
  Esc         — Exit screensaver


═══════════════════════════════════════════════
 TROUBLESHOOTING
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

"App is damaged and can't be opened" (macOS):
  Open Terminal and run:
    xattr -cr "/Applications/Aero Atlas — Terminal Time.app"
  Then try launching again.

Fonts look wrong / missing:
  The app bundles all fonts — no internet connection needed.
  If fonts appear missing, try quitting and relaunching.

Clock shows wrong timezone:
  The home city is auto-detected from your system timezone.
  If incorrect, open settings and adjust your city list.

Performance / stuttering:
  The split-flap animation is GPU-accelerated. On older machines,
  try closing other apps. The screensaver uses minimal CPU at rest.
