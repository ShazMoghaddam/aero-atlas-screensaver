 AERO ATLAS — TERMINAL TIME
 Atmospheric Split-Flap World Clock Screensaver
────────────────────────────────────────────────

OVERVIEW
────────
Terminal Time is a fullscreen screensaver inspired by the iconic
split-flap (Solari) departure boards found in airports and train
stations. It shows live world clock times for up to 10 cities,
with authentic flip animations, ambient click sounds, and five
colour accent themes.

FEATURES
────────
  ✦ Live world clocks — up to 10 cities simultaneously
  ✦ Authentic split-flap flip animation with physics bounce
  ✦ Idle ghost-flips — the board cycles through characters at rest
  ✦ 5 colour themes: Red, Amber, Green, Blue, Platinum
  ✦ Optional acoustic click sounds (3 profiles)
  ✦ Auto-detects your local timezone for the home city
  ✦ 300+ world cities included
  ✦ 12 / 24 hour display
  ✦ Works offline — fully self-contained, no internet needed
  ✦ Runs on macOS 10.15+ and Windows 10/11

SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS
───────────────────
  macOS:   10.15 Catalina or later (Intel + Apple Silicon)
  Windows: Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit)
  RAM:     256 MB minimum
  GPU:     Any GPU with basic hardware acceleration
